Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Zuppi is a flashcard app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Zuppi.

The short version: Zuppi has no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking, or developer server. Your cards stay on your devices and, if you use iCloud, sync only through your own private iCloud database. We cannot read them.

Information stored by Zuppi

Zuppi stores the content you create or import, including:

  • Decks and subdecks
  • Cards, notes, images, and audio
  • Review history and scheduling information
  • App settings and preferences

This information is stored on your device. If iCloud is available and enabled, it also syncs through your private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit. Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy apply to that storage.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. In particular, Zuppi does not include:

  • User accounts or sign-up
  • Analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • Advertising or tracking
  • A developer-operated server for your cards

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is optional. When enabled, your data is stored in your private iCloud database and is associated with your Apple Account. The developer does not have access to that private database and cannot read your cards.

You can turn off iCloud sync or remove iCloud data through your device settings and the app's data controls.

Purchases

Zuppi Premium is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. Apple handles payment details, receipts, refunds, and taxes. We do not receive your payment card information. Apple may provide us with sales and transaction information in aggregated form.

Apple Intelligence

On supported devices, Zuppi can use Apple Intelligence to create or simplify flashcards. These features run on your device using Apple's on-device models. Card text is not sent to a developer server.

Data deletion

You can delete your data from within Zuppi using Settings → Delete All Data. This removes your local data and requests deletion of the associated iCloud records.

Deleting the app removes local data from that device. iCloud data may remain in your Apple Account until it is deleted from within the app or through your iCloud settings.

Children

Zuppi is a general education app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not operate accounts or collect personal data, there is no personal information for us to retain about any user.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date on this page. Material changes will be communicated in the app or on this page.
